Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 13, 2026

1. Custom Software & Web Development Projects

Because of the custom nature of our technology systems, web applications, and UI/UX design services, we cannot offer full refunds once work has commenced. Payments made for project milestones that have already been delivered or worked upon are strictly non-refundable.

2. Subscription & Monthly Retainers

For monthly services such as AI Automation packages (AI Starter, AI Growth, AI Pro) and Maintenance & Care packages, you may cancel your subscription at any time. Cancellations will be effective at the end of the current billing cycle. We do not provide prorated refunds for partial months of service.

3. Exceptions & Special Circumstances

Refunds may be considered on a case-by-case basis under the following circumstances:

  • If an accidental duplicate payment was made.
  • If you request a cancellation before any work or planning has officially started on your project.

4. How to Request a Cancellation

To cancel a monthly plan or discuss project terms, please contact your dedicated project manager or email us at ntosweb@gmail.com. Please allow up to 7 business days for refund processing in eligible cases.
